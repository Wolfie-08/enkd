# How much does a Telegram shop bot cost in Uzbekistan?

*What you get, how it works, how long it takes, and what you pay. With live examples.*

The price of a Telegram shop bot is agreed on a short call, because it depends on catalogue size, languages, and whether the bot connects to your warehouse or ERP. Full implementation, with catalogue, cart, orders and an admin panel, takes **2–3 weeks**. Using Telegram itself is free.

## What is a Telegram shop bot?

A Telegram shop bot is a store that lives inside Telegram. Customers browse products, add them to a cart and place an order without leaving the chat, and your team manages everything from a browser admin panel.

In Uzbekistan this matters because your customers are already in Telegram. They do not need to install an app or remember a website.

## How does it work?

It works in three parts:

1. **The storefront.** A Mini App inside Telegram with photos, prices, cart and checkout. For phones that cannot open Mini Apps, the same catalogue works with plain buttons.
2. **The admin panel.** A browser page where your team adds products, changes prices, uploads photos and handles orders. A price changed in the panel appears in the bot immediately.
3. **The backend.** A server and database that hold products and orders, send order notifications to your team, and connect to your warehouse or ERP if you have one.

Everything is in Uzbek and Russian, kept in sync, so every product and every button exists in both languages.

## How long does it take?

A shop bot takes **2–3 weeks** from the first call to launch.

- **Week 1:** catalogue structure, languages, order flow, and who receives orders.
- **Week 2:** storefront, admin panel and notifications running on a test bot.
- **Week 3, launch:** your products loaded, then a short period of fixes after real customers use it.

The usual delay is on the content side: product photos, names and prices in both languages.

## How much does it cost?

The price is agreed on a call, after I understand the scope. What moves it:

- **Catalogue size and structure.** Categories, variants and sizes.
- **Connection to a warehouse or ERP.** Live stock and prices take more work than a manually managed catalogue.
- **Payments.** Online payment adds integration work compared with orders confirmed by phone.
- **Extras.** Banners, discounts, broadcasts to customers, a customer-service bot.

After launch you pay for the server, plus support if you want it. The Telegram Bot API costs nothing.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between a Mini App and a normal bot?

A Mini App is a small web page that opens inside Telegram, with a real catalogue layout and photos. A normal bot uses buttons and messages. A good shop bot offers both, so older phones still work.

### Can the bot use my existing warehouse stock and prices?

Yes. At Supply Group the storefront reads the live warehouse catalogue, so customers only see what is in stock, at the current price.

### Who adds products and changes prices?

Your team does, in the admin panel. No developer is needed for day-to-day changes.

### Does it support Uzbek and Russian?

Yes. Every product and every button is kept in both languages. Customers pick their language.

### Can I send promotions to my customers?

Yes. A broadcast feature sends a message to everyone who has started the bot. Supply Group runs a separate marketing-broadcast bot for this.

### Can AI answer customer questions?

Yes. An AI layer can answer questions about products and orders from your own catalogue, with a handover to a person when it is unsure.
