*What you get, why search and AI engines need to read it, how long it takes, and what you pay.*

The price of a custom business website is agreed on a short call, because it depends on pages, languages, and whether you need accounts or an admin panel. Full implementation takes **2–3 weeks**. After launch you pay only for the domain and hosting. Below: what is included, how it gets built, and where the money goes.

## What should a business website include in 2026?

A business website in 2026 should be fast, available in the languages your clients speak, and readable by both Google and AI assistants such as ChatGPT and Perplexity.

In practice that means:

- **Fast pages.** The pages are pre-built, so they load instantly, even on mobile data.
- **Two or three languages.** Uzbek and English, or Uzbek and Russian, with every page kept in sync.
- **Structured data.** Hidden, machine-readable facts about your business, services and FAQ, so search and AI engines can quote you correctly.
- **Forms that reach you.** Requests go to your inbox or Telegram, not into a dashboard nobody checks.
- **An admin panel only where needed.** For news, applications or products, not for a page that changes once a year.

## Why does structured data matter now?

More clients now ask an AI assistant instead of scrolling Google results. Structured data is how you become the answer it gives. It tells engines, in a format they trust, who you are, where you work, what you offer and what your FAQ says.

A site without it can still rank, but it is much harder for an AI to describe your business accurately.

## How does the build work?

It works in four steps:

1. **Content first.** We agree on the pages, the questions your clients ask, and the text in each language.
2. **Design and build.** Next.js with pre-built pages, deployed on Vercel.
3. **Search setup.** Structured data, sitemap, language tags, share images, and Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
4. **Handover.** You get the domain, the code and the accounts in your name.

## How long does it take?

A business website takes **2–3 weeks**.

- **Week 1:** pages, languages and text.
- **Week 2:** design, build and review.
- **Week 3, launch:** domain connected, search setup done, forms tested.

The usual delay is text and photos, especially translations.

## How much does it cost?

The price is agreed on a call, after I understand the scope. What moves it:

- **Number of pages and languages.** Each language is a full copy to write and check.
- **Accounts and applications.** Sign-in, uploads and review panels add the most work.
- **Admin panel.** Needed only if your team updates content often.
- **Content.** Whether you provide the text and photos, or they need to be written.

After launch, running costs are a `.uz` domain plus hosting.

## FAQ

### Is a custom site better than Tilda or Wix?

For a simple page, a builder is fine. A custom site is worth it when you need speed, several languages kept in sync, structured data, or accounts and applications.

### Can I edit the content myself?

Yes, if you need to. Pages that change often get an admin panel; pages that change once a year are edited on request.

### Do you do SEO?

The technical part, yes: speed, structured data, sitemap, language tags and search console setup. Ranking over time also depends on content and on other sites mentioning yours.

### Will I own the site?

Yes. The domain, code and hosting account are in your name.

### Can the site connect to my Telegram bot or CRM?

Yes. Form requests can go to Telegram, and a site can share products and orders with a Telegram shop bot.
