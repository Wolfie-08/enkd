# studio.enkd.uz: BRIEF

Interviewed 2026-09-29 (four decisions answered by the owner in chat). Items marked
**[authored]** were not asked and are my decisions, based on the owner's request text,
the seven reference videos (`docs/studio/reference-videos/`) and the customer research
(`docs/studio/customer-research.md`).

## What it is, for whom

Enkd Studio builds digital systems for small and medium businesses in Uzbekistan:
AI automation, AI agents, AI chatbots, dashboards (analytics), CRM systems and
motion-design promo videos. Buyers are Tashkent owners and managers in distribution,
Telegram-first retail, and clinics: people whose orders live in Telegram DMs and
whose reports live in Excel.

- **Believe by the end [authored]:** "These people can turn my messy inbox into a system,
  and I just watched their AI actually answer me."
- **One action [authored]:** book the free audit. Label everywhere: UZ "Bepul audit",
  RU "Бесплатный аудит", EN "Free audit". Goes to Telegram + the contact form.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe (owner):** "Light glass SaaS". References: Taskip promo (V7), the isometric
   dashboard explainer (V5), the tilted dashboard flythrough (V6). Frosted glass,
   pale mint air, UI floating in 3D.
2. **Journey (owner's request, sequenced by me):** one customer message travels through
   the business: it arrives, piles up with the others, an AI agent answers it, it becomes
   a deal in the CRM, automations move it, it lands on the dashboard, and the promo video
   brings the next one in.
3. **Energy [authored]:** calm open, uneasy second beat (the pile of unanswered DMs),
   quiet and intimate at the agent (the peak), brisk through CRM/automation, broad and
   confident at the dashboard, bright at the promo, still at the close.
4. **Feeling (owner chose the moment):** the peak is the **live AI agent demo**.
5. **What no other site does [authored from 4]:** the visitor talks to a real agent that
   lives on a glass pane inside the 3D world, and the answer is about *their* business.
6. **Range:** premium-minimal, light. Not dark, not maximalist.
7. **Structure (owner):** **one unbroken world.** Worldflight mode, one continuous Blender
   camera move cut into legs (seams are pixel-identical by construction).
8. **Assets:** no footage or photos. The world is rendered in Blender; the UI screens on the
   glass panes are rendered with Remotion; the promo reel is a HyperFrames composition.

## Grammar

**2.4 Continuous world.** The journey has real geography (a message moving through a
system), and the owner asked for one unbroken world. Why not the others: filmic one-shot
would pin acts and cut; chaptered editorial and typographic poster have no 3D world;
live surface forbids marketing chrome and the owner wants a sales page; gallery reads as
a catalog of services, which is exactly the template the research says competitors
already ship; split stage fits before/after but not six stations; cutlist is youth-energy.

Nav is a **map**: a route rail of the six stations (jumps to each), plus language and CTA.

## Feeling curve

```
0  Curiosity    a sunlit glass world, one orange message drifting in from far away
1  Recognition  dozens of grey unanswered bubbles hanging in the air, the orange one lost among them
2  Intimacy     everything goes still at one frosted pane, and it talks back to you   (PEAK)
3  Momentum     the message becomes a card sliding across a pipeline of glass lanes
4  Relief       rails light up and small orbs carry work between stations on their own
5  Clarity      the camera rises over one tilted dashboard where every branch is on one screen
6  Delight      a glass screen playing the promo, colour arriving in a pale world
7  Resolve      the camera pulls back to the whole world at rest; one offer, one button
```

## The peak

> "I typed that I run a dental clinic, and the glass screen inside the site told me
> exactly what it would automate for me."

Lives in leg 2. Largest weight + linger on the track. Leg 1 before it is the quiet,
uneasy one (grey bubbles, no accent), so the stillness at the pane reads as a change.

## Tell-someone sentence

It's the site where **you fly into a glass world and an AI on one of the panes answers
questions about your own business.**

## Authored silence

The stretch between the hero fading and the leg-1 copy window (track ~0.07 to 0.11) is
intentional air: the camera is travelling toward the bubble cloud with nothing on screen.
