## Web-build notes

1. **One persistent card set across sections 1–7.** The same DOM cards (hero, deck, cascade, 3x2 grid) are re-laid-out with GSAP Flip or a scroll-scrubbed transform timeline. Do not remount cards per section. That continuity is the signature of this site.
2. **Hero card fan:** 7 cards, `transform-origin: 50% 100%`. Fan angles run about -9° to +7° with a step of ~2.5° and x-spacing ~70 px at 1440 w. Entry: lead card from y +320 px, rotate 25° and scale 1.15, settling with `back.out(1.4)` over ~0.9 s. The others stagger out from behind it at ~90 ms each. Scroll-scrub the collapse to a stack (scale 0.35, x to 0) over the first ~30% of the pinned range.
3. **Word-by-word reveal:** split headings to word spans. Each starts at `opacity .25; filter: blur(6px); color: #a9a7ab` and ends at `opacity 1; blur 0; color #0b0a0c`. Duration ~0.5 s, stagger ~70–80 ms, `power3.out`. Use `ScrollTrigger scrub 0.5` when the heading is pinned, or a time-based reveal when it enters. Eyebrow and body fade in 150–250 ms after the last word.
4. **Pinned sections + parallax lag:** pin each section for ~100–150 vh. Cards travel at ~0.6x scroll speed on exit, so a card stays stuck at the top-right over the nav for 4–5 s (Shots 2–4).
5. **Colour blocking:** heading colours change mid-headline (crimson line 2, mint "unique", grey "best."). Store as spans with data-tone, not separate elements.
6. **Chat bubbles:** `scale 0 → 1` from the tail origin with `back.out(2)` in ~0.45 s, staggered ~150 ms, then an idle float (`y ±4 px`, ~3 s sine, random phase). Bubble colours per handle: blue, mint, dark red, black, amber, orange.
7. **Physics icon piles:** use Matter.js or Rapier with 8–10 bodies. Trigger at ~30% section visibility. Gravity 1, restitution ~0.3, friction 0.4. Render each glyph in a soft white radial glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px 8px #fff`). The pile lives in "Our vision" (left) and in the yellow band (bottom).
