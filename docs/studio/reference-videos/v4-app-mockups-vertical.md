## Web/Blender build notes
- **Mockup shots are just a screenshot on a plane:** in Blender, photoscan-style desk scene (or an HDRI + plane desk), phone = rounded-rect with 0.02 bevel and Principled BSDF (metallic 1, roughness .35, color #2A2A2C), screen = emission (strength 1.0) UI PNG. Camera 50-85 mm, f/2.0, DoF focus on screen, slow dolly 6-8% over 1.5 s (`ease-in-out`).
- **Hard-cut cadence:** ~1.5 s per device shot, 1.9 s at the hero close-up, 4 shots + 1 reveal = 10 s; good rhythm for a vertical Reels/Shorts hero loop or a scroll section.
- **Title reveal:** opacity 0->1, y +12 px -> 0, 0.3 s `power2.out`; subtitle 0.15 s later; ghost-out at end (opacity to 0.2 + blur 6 px in 0.4 s). For the CSS version: `text-shadow: 0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,.35)` and `clamp(2rem, 6vw, 3.25rem)` bold Inter.
- **Chart bars growing on phone:** animate `scaleY` 0->1, 0.8 s `power3.out`, 40 ms stagger, line draws with `stroke-dashoffset`.
- **Floating icons reveal (shot 6):** 6-7 rounded-square 3D-ish icons (CSS `translateZ` + drop shadow `0 20px 30px rgba(0,0,0,.18)`), lift from screen position: y +80 -> 0, scale .4 -> 1, rotate +/-8 deg, `back.out(1.6)`, 0.06 s stagger, then perpetual float `sine.inOut` 3 s yoyo, 8-14 px.
- **Hero idea for enkd:** laptop-on-desk hero where the real site UI is the laptop screen texture; on scroll, dolly out (FOV 30 -> 45) while project icons pop out and orbit; title swaps from white to dark as the background brightens (contrast switch at the last shot).
- **Warm palette for the room:** cream #EFEEE5, taupe #C8BDAA, wood #876B4B, wall #947A5B, screen white #F6F6F6, accent blue #2F6BD8; keep highlights soft (sun 3200 K, size 3 m, strength 3).
- **Generation tip:** AI video models garble UI text; generate the device shot with a blank screen, then composite real UI screenshots and the real title strings as overlays.
